Where the files in assets/brand/ come from (copied 2026-10-23). Use them as they are; never redraw them.

Logo/cpfs-symbol-favicon.svg
Logo/cpfs-symbol-on-dark.svg
Logo/cpfs-symbol-on-light.svg
  From the CyClone Power brand project (CPBS), folder cpfs/logo/. Copied byte for byte.
  Path logo, palette and dark theme approved by the owner on 2026-10-23.
  src/Cpfs/Brand.xaml transcribes the two symbol SVGs into XAML drawings with the same coordinates, colours and strokes.

Logo/cpfs-app-icon.svg
Logo/cpfs-app-icon-16.svg
Logo/cpfs-app-icon-20.svg
Logo/cpfs-app-icon-24.svg
  The app icon source ("Tile": the Path mark in Ink on a Tangerine rounded square), from CPBS cpfs/logo/,
  copied byte for byte. Approved by the brand (CPBS) on 2026-10-23. cpfs-app-icon.svg is the 256 px master;
  the 16, 20 and 24 px files are hand-tuned frames for those sizes.
  src/Cpfs/Brand.xaml transcribes cpfs-app-icon-20.svg for the title bar icon.

cpfs.ico
  The 16, 20 and 24 px entries rendered from their own frames (Logo/cpfs-app-icon-16.svg, -20.svg, -24.svg), and
  the 32, 40, 48, 64 and 256 px entries from Logo/cpfs-app-icon.svg, with a build-time tool (Svg.Skia 5.2.3 and
  SkiaSharp, both MIT) that is not part of this repository and not shipped.
  Entries below 256 px are 32-bit DIBs; the 256 px entry is PNG.

  The lockup (Path symbol and wordmark), from CPBS cpfs/logo/, copied byte for byte on 2026-10-24.
